# Client confirmation of chargeable variation

Status: proposed wording, not legally approved or implemented.
Updated 7 September 2026. Assumes England and Wales; jurisdiction and the
underlying contract must be checked before live use.

## Intended effect

Extend the existing variation flow for work undertaken without written
confirmation. Obtain client-side site-manager sign-off acknowledging both the
variation and its chargeability, rather than a mere record of attendance.
This is distinct from PM acknowledgement of receiving an ordinary report.

## Proposed statement

> I confirm that I am authorised to agree this variation on behalf of
> [client legal name]. On behalf of that client, I confirm and accept the work
> described in [variation reference and scope] as a variation to the original
> scope under [contract/project reference]. I agree that this work is additionally
> chargeable by [Leodis contracting entity] and that the client will pay for it
> using the rates and charging basis set out in [attached schedule reference,
> version and date], which I have been given and accept. The final amount will
> be calculated from the quantities, time and other chargeable items recorded
> and valued in accordance with that agreed basis. Payment will be made under
> the payment terms of the contract.

Use an agreed-price alternative where appropriate, instead of conflicting price
and rate statements. Do not use this template unchanged if the contract requires
a different valuation or variation procedure. It is not a personal guarantee by
the signer and does not by itself guarantee recovery of an invoice.

## Required signing record

- Correct contracting entities and project/contract reference.
- Specific variation scope and evidence, who instructed it and when, and dates
  of work. Do not backdate the signature to the instruction or work date.
- Identified pricing schedule available before signing, including applicable
  materials/plant/markups and VAT treatment; no unshown or unspecified rates.
- Signer's name, employer, position, contact details and authority confirmation.
  Verify authority through the contract/client arrangements; a checkbox alone
  cannot confer authority a person does not have.
- Separate signature explicitly attached to this commercial statement, not a
  reused report signature or a receipt acknowledgement.
- Immutable signed scope, statement version, pricing attachment and timestamp.
  Material amendments require renewed agreement. Make a copy available to client.
- If signature or authority is unavailable, preserve the factual record as
  awaiting commercial confirmation and flag it to the office; never label it agreed.

## Review before live use

Have a construction solicitor check the statement against Leodis's contracts,
including permitted signatories, written-instruction requirements, notice/time
limits, retrospective confirmation, valuation and payment procedures. A signature
does not automatically cure an unmet contractual condition.
